# Research Material Announcements and Disclosure Artifacts

KZO-247 adds issuer-owned announcement evidence and retained disclosure artifacts to Taiwan research. The public surface has no UI, portfolio access, transaction behavior, sentiment feed, or upstream refresh tool. Existing research identity, financial-statement, and price tools retain their responsibilities.

## Public read contracts

Both tools require explicit `research:read` consent and the enabled research tool group. A portfolio-only connection does not grant research access. Canonical evidence is returned in `structuredContent.result`; text content contains a compact status summary. The tools are read-only, non-destructive, idempotent, closed-world, and forbid background task execution.

| Tool | Initial request | Bounds and result |
|---|---|---|
| `list_material_announcements` | One Listing selector and required fixed `context`; optional publication range, narrower event-date range, evidence view, purposes, order and limit | Default 90-day publication window; maximum two calendar years. Default 25 and maximum 100 complete announcement records. Descending publication order by default, with stable ID tie-breaking. |
| `get_disclosure_artifact` | Listing selector, required fixed `context` and opaque retained artifact ID; optional evidence view, purposes, order and limit | Default three and maximum ten pages; at most 50,000 exposed Unicode characters. Retained blocks and separately verified claims, with page/total coverage and truncation. |

The Listing selector is `listing_id` or `ticker_venue` with a lossless ticker and explicit `TWSE` or `TPEX` venue. Disclosures belong to the resolved Issuer. The selector is not permission to follow a venue transfer or attach evidence to another issuer.

Initial requests must include `context.knowledgeAt`. Omitted `effectiveAt` defaults to `knowledgeAt`, and omitted `assessmentMode` defaults to `effective`; `re_evaluate` requires `policySetVersion`. The service returns the normalized fixed context. Continuations use only the repeated subject and cursor.

Publication bounds determine when an announcement became knowable. Event dates may narrow that range but do not replace it. Missing, blank or invalid optional event dates become `null` without failing an otherwise valid snapshot or detail record; original event-date strings remain in `rawEventDate`. Event-range filters exclude records with unknown event dates. Required publication dates and times remain strict. Snapshot revision identity includes the retained source fields and attachment descriptors, so changed event dates or rule clauses cannot reuse stale successful detail during a failed refresh. The service preserves publication precision, original publication fields, title, rule clause, event date, issuer explanation, attachment references, and correction/retraction relations.

Explanation text is limited to 20,000 Unicode characters per announcement. The response states original and retained counts, exact truncation, source location, hash and source URL. An explanation artifact permits inspection beyond the inline limit. Dataset tools do not translate, summarize, assign sentiment, or classify catalysts.

Both initial requests accept `evidenceView`, defaulting to `selected_with_conflicts`; `all_observations` explicitly requests retained audit history. The default announcement view excludes explicitly superseded observations while retaining unresolved equal-authority conflict participants. Selection metadata records selected/conflicting IDs, excluded counts, reasons and policy version. The effective `relationIndex` includes correction/retraction effects only from available, non-superseded notices in either evidence view. Supersession edges preserve historical retirement through a revision chain. Raw `item.relations` remains retained audit evidence, including obsolete notice wording, and does not independently invalidate report targets. Effective relations remain available across page boundaries. Every entry in `relationIndex`, `unresolvedRelationIndex` and `unknownRelationIndex` carries a `provenanceId` backed by the same page’s deduplicated `provenance` records, including source notices outside the publication window. Provenance is rebuilt from surviving items and effective index sources after response-budget trimming. Reports preserve these IDs and render source URL, content hash and parser version for notices whose source item is not displayed; cross-page source/provenance mappings must remain consistent.

Optional `purposes` accepts at most twenty unique IDs from the versioned registry advertised by the manifest: `factual_use`, `current_assessment`, and `exhaustive_conclusion`. Omission evaluates all three. The response reports readiness separately for each requested purpose. Evidence view and purposes are bound into the continuation chain.

### Continuations and budgets

Continue with only the repeated `subject` and `cursor`. Do not repeat or change context, artifact ID, range, ordering, or limit. A signed cursor binds the immutable Listing, normalized query, fixed temporal context, versions, operation and authenticated connector context. Artifact cursors also bind content hash and extraction version. Chains expire after 24 hours.

Each response includes `page.continuity`: `queryHash`, `offset`, `returnedCount`, `totalCount`, and `requestCursor`. Counts cover the authorized, filtered selection; artifact counts cover retained numbered pages available to the read. Initial pages start at offset zero with a null request cursor. Subsequent pages must match the preceding continuation cursor and advance by the actual returned count, preserving query identity and total count. Terminal cursors must agree with the count boundary.

Report composition checks the complete supplied chain from its initial page, including artifact page-number order. A valid unfinished chain remains partial; terminal-only, skipped, duplicate or inconsistent pages are rejected. A continuation response describes only its own page: even the terminal page remains partial and cannot independently establish exhaustive conclusions. A report may establish complete aggregate coverage after validating the entire chain against the requested window. These checks establish structural consistency of retained responses, not authenticity of wholly caller-forged report data. Added metadata remains subject to the final response-byte budget.

Available-artifact admission validates each page against the 50,000-character budget using the combined Unicode count of stored blocks and verified claims. Verification must leave room for its claim text; an oversized append is rejected before either persistence backend mutates the batch, preserving prior immutable evidence. Reads budget only blocks and claims eligible for that subject and knowledge cutoff, and report the exact emitted character count. Legacy oversized records require bounded re-verification or re-extraction; they do not become successful empty reads. Character caps and serialized response-byte budgets are separate. Responses preserve complete records and expose response-budget truncation; an oversized indivisible record returns `record_too_large`. Callers must not interpret an incomplete page chain as exhaustive evidence.

Announcement selection first reads publication/event-window metadata and relevant same-collection or incoming-lineage companions, without transferring explanation or attachment payloads. Out-of-window corrections remain able to invalidate queried evidence; unrelated history does not enter query counts or conflict reasons. Incoming lineage traversal also retains relevant ambiguous notices and their later superseding revisions under the same issuer, Listing and temporal constraints. Only the selected page’s IDs (at most 100) are then loaded as full records. Continuity hashes bind the fixed query, immutable record/provenance identity and deterministically ordered relevant lineage metadata; unrelated history does not change the chain.

### Artifact authorization and exposure

An artifact must be referenced by the selected issuer's retained announcement attachment or independently retained material-reference record. Arbitrary URLs, unrelated IDs, generic provenance objects and financial-statement bulk XBRL are rejected. The material-reference seam does not add investor-material discovery.

Artifact reads select the requested opaque ID at the persistence boundary, together with issuer and temporal constraints, before loading its payload. Listing/reference authorization remains a separate scoped existence check against the exact announcement attachment or investor-material reference; it does not load issuer-wide announcement or material payloads. Unrelated issuer artifacts and their retained bytes are not loaded for a public read. Reads never fetch a source, extract a file, append evidence, enqueue work, populate a cache or change freshness. Restricted, indeterminate and failed extraction states preserve safe metadata while withholding bulk content. When the artifact has not yet been retained, authorization checks scoped membership in both announcement and investor-material references; retained artifacts still require their exact recorded reference kind and ID. Missing retained content may return a typed acquisition state when a valid reference exists; a store failure returns a tool error rather than empty evidence.

Blocks retain source page/table location, subject, period, unit and extraction context. Unknown period/unit remains explicitly unknown. Raw extraction or OCR does not become a verified publisher claim automatically. Claim exposure requires matching subject, cited blocks, location, qualifiers and knowledge cutoff. Retained source bytes are internal; they are not included in public MCP output.

## Acquisition and persistence

Research-enabled pg-boss startup registers `research-disclosure-acquisition`, runs it at startup, and schedules `*/15 * * * *`. Startup and scheduled jobs share one singleton key. The research worker uses a twelve-hour execution expiry for sequential board-wide detail and attachment work. Job cancellation propagates to source requests and is checked between processing stages and before scan persistence; an expired or stopped run does not continue as an ordinary source failure. Internal ingestion uses official MOPS-origin routes for both boards:

- TWSE: `https://openapi.twse.com.tw/v1/opendata/t187ap04_L`
- TPEx: `https://www.tpex.org.tw/openapi/v1/mopsfin_t187ap04_O`

Snapshot rows with a syntactically valid ticker absent from the board’s identity catalog are skipped without attribution; they do not fail collection for recognized issuers. Known eligible rows still require a unique effective identity mapping, and malformed routing keys remain errors.

For each recognized snapshot record, ingestion also uses the verified official MOPS SPA history and detail routes, `https://mops.twse.com.tw/mops/api/t05st01` and `https://mops.twse.com.tw/mops/api/t05st01_detail`. It verifies issuer/board/date/sequence routing before retaining issuer-authored detail, attachment references and explicit correction links. History and detail times must match the retained publication precision: minute-level records match within the same minute, while second-level records require exact seconds. History matching must remain unique; enrichment preserves the original timestamp and precision. A denied or malformed detail response is recorded in independent `detailQuality`; usable snapshot explanation survives. Correction/retraction lineage is still resolved from exact citations in that retained snapshot text when possible, without treating failed detail acquisition as successful. Permitted delegated-subsidiary prefixes, including bounded named or quoted subsidiaries before the first `公告`, share explicit correction/retraction detection; incidental wording later in a subject does not establish a notice. Citation parsing preserves explicit title/date pairs, excludes date-like content inside quoted titles, and leaves unclear associations unresolved. Candidate lookup may use a coarse union of those selectors, but final lineage matching requires an actual publisher pair. Supported citation dates normalize consistently: Gregorian and ROC slash, hyphen, and Chinese year/month/day forms resolve to the same publication day, with calendar validation and unchanged exact-title, listing, venue, and ambiguity checks.

These feeds are recent snapshots, not a two-year history API. A successful fetch does not establish historical or attachment-discovery completeness. Native field differences include TWSE's trailing-space `主旨 ` key and TPEx's `SecuritiesCompanyCode`; publication times may omit a leading zero. Snapshot, history and detail parsing share explicit clock forms: H:mm/HH:mm and three/four compact digits retain minute precision, while H:mm:ss/HH:mm:ss and five/six compact digits retain second precision. Original source clocks remain unchanged.

Acquisition validates official source URLs, rejects redirects, bounds fetch time and input size, hashes retained content, and performs extraction before persistence. Official snapshot/history/detail JSON uses strict UTF-8 decoding and hashes the received bytes; malformed byte sequences are processing failures, never replacement-text evidence. When transport MIME is missing or generic, a PDF byte signature or supported official attachment filename/URL metadata selects the extractor; hinted text must pass strict decoding/control checks and hinted HTML must have a recognized document signature. Text decoding honors a byte-order mark, otherwise uses a supported transport charset (including Big5), then bounded HTML metadata detection or UTF-8 when no transport encoding is declared. Unsupported or malformed declarations and invalid byte sequences fail extraction instead of retaining replacement text; unsupported non-UTF-8 XML declarations without transport/BOM guidance are rejected. Decoded access-denial documents remain restricted. Unknown binary content remains unsupported. The artifact retains its resolved `mediaType` and `sourceMediaType` (the received transport type, or the generic default when absent). Plain text uses bounded logical pages. HTML extraction splits long text and tables into bounded logical pages while preserving Unicode text and stable table identifiers; ordinary table rows stay together, and oversized rows continue as exact text chunks. PDF extraction uses physical page numbers. A physical page exceeding 50,000 Unicode characters or 180 KiB of serialized text fails extraction with `disclosure_extraction_physical_page_limit`; ingestion records `processing_failed` and operator recovery rather than an unreadable available artifact. It does not split physical pages into invented page locations. A textless PDF page is confirmed empty only when its operator list contains known nonpainting operations, including text/font and graphics-state setup. Text-showing, painting, compositing and unknown operations do not prove a page empty. Image, vector and other unresolved textless pages remain extraction gaps; legacy artifacts without confirmed-empty metadata retain conservative gap handling. Logical text/HTML locations must not be represented as physical PDF pages. Extraction retains evidence without promoting it to verified claims. Every scheduled scan revalidates external attachment bytes. Attachment content identity includes its hash, extraction version and MIME metadata; content changes create a new parent observation and artifact, while unchanged content replays idempotently. A failed refresh creates an unresolved attachment reference in a new parent observation and records the failed attempt, preserving older successful evidence only in history. Repeated failures remain idempotent and successful recovery creates a new usable revision. Failed acquisition attempts are not fabricated empty artifacts. Recognized MOPS access-denial documents remain restricted even when the server returns HTTP 200 or advertises an attachment MIME type. Collection, detail and attachment paths check these denial markers before parsing or extraction; a later permitted successful retry may retain the actual document.

Migration `124_research_disclosures.sql` adds announcement, artifact, scan and material-reference tables under `research`. Migration `125_research_disclosure_scan_lookup.sql` adds indexes for latest scans per issuer/Listing/venue, successful scans, and artifact-attempt membership. Migration `126_research_disclosure_announcement_lookup.sql` adds indexes for scoped announcement windows, collection identity, successful detail selection, attachment membership and incoming lineage. Migration `127_research_disclosure_unresolved_lineage.sql` indexes explicit ambiguous-target membership. Migration `128_research_disclosure_unknown_targets.sql` indexes scoped notices whose target is not identified. Memory and Postgres implementations expose the same append/read contract. Consecutive replay of the same active observation is idempotent; conflicting reuse of an immutable ID is rejected. Changed detail, including a return to earlier content, creates a distinct observation bound to its active predecessors and fresh provenance IDs, then supersedes those predecessors. Historical evidence remains unchanged. Failed detail fetches can reuse only the unique active successful detail for that collection, so they cannot revive retired evidence. Corrections and retractions preserve old evidence and append relations. Report conclusions depending on corrected or retracted evidence are withheld until supported by the replacement evidence.

Acquisition resolves revisions through exact collection identity or a verified publisher record identity derived from the MOPS market, company, entry date and sequence parameters, and loads at most 100 active predecessor records for content comparison. Shared titles and publication timestamps do not establish revision identity; observations remain separate when verified publisher identity is unavailable. Unsupported lineage fails closed; failed detail acquisition cannot select among ambiguous active predecessors. A revision cycle, too many active predecessors or unresolved predecessor payload records marks the affected row attempt and Listing scan as failed while remaining board rows continue. A later successful row cannot erase that Listing failure. The board outcome signals partial failure without marking unrelated Listing scans as failed; cancellation and persistence failures retain their existing propagation. Correction/retraction resolution queries exact quoted-title and publication-day candidates after the official detail is fetched; it reuses that response and preserves ambiguity instead of truncating matches. Citation candidates include scoped metadata for incoming supersession chains. Matching collapses explicitly retired observations before uniqueness checks; a changed-title successor retires the old match without being silently selected as the cited target. Relevant lineage cycles remain unresolved, and genuinely distinct active matches remain ambiguous. Multiple active exact matches are retained as sorted candidate IDs in `unresolvedRelations`, separate from resolved links. The page-scoped `unresolvedRelationIndex` identifies candidate announcements affected by active available notices, including relevant notices outside the requested publication window. Dependent announcement and attachment-parent judgments remain withheld; unrelated evidence and scan readiness remain usable. Explicitly superseding a notice retires its ambiguity, while the replacement’s resolved links retain their own effect. If no target can be identified, `unknownRelationTargets` and the page-scoped `unknownRelationIndex` preserve notice kind and publication context without inventing candidate IDs or resolved links. Potentially earlier same-Listing announcement or attachment-parent interpretations are withheld as `unknown_correction_scope`, while original source support, later independent evidence and scan status remain usable. Equal second timestamps and uncertain minute/date ordering are treated conservatively, with date boundaries in Taiwan time. Resolution or explicit supersession removes the unknown-scope limitation. SQL and memory use the same whitespace normalization for citation titles.

Publication/effective time and retrieval/processing/knowledge time are independently constrained. Later evidence must not appear in an earlier knowledge-time report. Parent-source provenance and retained artifact provenance must remain distinguishable so artifact bytes, hashes, extraction versions and acquisition records can be audited.

## Readiness and focused reports

Manifest and announcement reads request at most the latest scan attempt and the latest successful scan from persistence, scoped to issuer, Listing, venue and both temporal cutoffs. PostgreSQL limits each selection before transferring scan payloads; memory clones only the selected records. Missing-artifact recovery separately requests the latest matching artifact attempt, so unrelated newer scans cannot hide an earlier processing failure or restriction. Full scan history remains retained for audit.

The official collection check is current through 30 minutes, indeterminate after 30 minutes through two hours, and stale after two hours. Scan `checkedAt`, `publicationEnd` and source `retrievedAt` record collection-response observation time; later detail or attachment work does not reset freshness. Completion is recorded separately in `processedAt` and `knowledgeAt`, so historical reads cannot see the finished scan before that processing cutoff. A failed request with no collection response records its failure completion time without claiming successful source coverage. Announcement observations themselves do not expire; freshness belongs to the collection check. Source failure does not refresh old evidence. A failed refresh preserves the most recent successful scan as the selected scan until its own freshness boundary. `scan.latestAttempt` independently exposes the failed attempt; current-assessment readiness is degraded while the selected successful scan remains current, then blocked when that scan becomes indeterminate or stale.

A current official scan is mandatory for current catalyst/risk assessment and the disclosure prerequisite of final recommendation. Artifact restrictions or missing pages withhold only dependent claims. A current scan with a failed attachment refresh reports degraded current-assessment readiness and an explicit reason; independent announcement text remains usable. Artifact claims must also fall within the report’s inclusive publication window. Announcement attachments require a returned, eligible parent announcement; unresolved corrections or restricted parent evidence withhold dependent claims, while inline explanation truncation alone does not disqualify separately verified artifact claims. Investor-material artifacts use their own publication time without inventing an announcement parent. Current interpretations of external artifacts additionally require a successful retained attempt for that artifact and source locator in the selected current official scan. An announcement leaving the recent snapshot therefore cannot keep its old attachment eligible for current analysis solely because an unrelated collection scan succeeded. Missing, failed or stale revalidation blocks dependent current interpretations while preserving verified historical facts and immutable artifact reads. Derived inline-explanation artifacts are exempt because they retain announcement text rather than independently fetched external content. Investor materials also require equivalent positive refresh evidence for current use; the material-reference seam alone does not supply it. A current but non-exhaustive scan cannot establish the absence of other announcements. Authority, confidence, completeness, freshness and purpose readiness are separate fields.

The focused report uses a fixed manifest context and bounded calls to the store-only services. Its standard scan is twelve months; its focused scan is ninety days and explicitly non-exhaustive. A standard report may request a 13–24 month extension with an explicit unresolved corporate-action, litigation, financing, restructuring or long-lived thesis reason and a named thesis item. Focused reports reject extensions. English and Traditional Chinese rendering preserve the same original evidence, classifications and withholding decisions.

Catalysts and risks distinguish observed, scheduled, conditional and speculative states. Their triggering evidence, causal mechanism, affected metric or assumption, horizon, confirmation and disconfirmation must be explicit. The factual statement must equal an exact retained publisher excerpt. Observed and scheduled statuses require matching dated publisher evidence and occurrence/scheduling cues. Tentative expected, anticipated, projected or forecast dates (including 預計、預估、預期) do not independently establish a firm schedule. Firm will/shall future assertions and explicit “is set for” or “is to be held on” constructions may support scheduled events, while future wording cannot establish observed events; negation, conditions and uncertainty still withhold either classification. Explicit three-digit ROC and four-digit Gregorian dates normalize paired hyphen/slash forms and Chinese year/month/day forms, with real calendar and leap-year validation. The original literal date must still occur in the exact publisher excerpt; normalization does not rewrite source wording. Classification checks the full source-derived sentence context of every exact excerpt occurrence, treating line wrapping as whitespace for sentence boundaries while preserving original source text. It conservatively withholds observed or scheduled status when that assertion context contains negation, cancellation, conditions or modal uncertainty; mixed or ambiguous prose may therefore remain withheld despite an affirmative keyword. Original evidence stays available. Mechanisms and other interpretation remain explicitly provisional analytical judgments, with source support recorded separately. Analyst interpretation is not a publisher Source Fact; an evidence ID alone does not establish that the proposed statement is true. Unsupported sentiment and directional investment instructions are outside this report. All six analyst-prose fields reject common affirmative and negated imperative/advisory trading forms in active or passive voice, including inflected and ticker/name-only instructions that omit a stock/share noun; renderer revalidation applies the same checks. Legitimate issuer operations such as buybacks, treasury-share financing and subsidiary acquisitions remain expressible, and exact publisher quotations remain source evidence. These wording checks do not claim exhaustive natural-language classification.

This focused profile does not evaluate the remaining mandatory financial, revenue, price and valuation gates. It therefore issues no final recommendation even when its disclosure prerequisite passes. Original publisher wording remains available alongside rendered analysis. Every rendered assessment retains deduplicated evidence references, including its status anchor and failed dependencies even when the judgment is withheld. English and Traditional Chinese labels identify these roles; announcement IDs and artifact/claim pairs remain literal escaped text. Rendered source text and dynamic metadata use literal punctuation encoding so embedded Markdown links, images, HTML and formatting cannot become active report content. Canonical structured evidence is unchanged.

## Operator recovery and rollback

The existing research gates remain default-off: `MCP_RESEARCH_ACQUISITION_ENABLED`, `MCP_RESEARCH_MCP_ENABLED`, and `MCP_RESEARCH_SKILL_ENABLED`. The independent `MCP_RESEARCH_ANNOUNCEMENTS_TWSE_ENABLED` and `MCP_RESEARCH_ANNOUNCEMENTS_TPEX_ENABLED` acquisition switches also default to false. A board's worker route requires its switch and the global acquisition gate. Turning a board's acquisition switch off preserves retained store reads. Enable acquisition and canonical identity readiness before exposing reads. Research reads do not offer an ad hoc refresh or extraction action.

For missing or stale scans, inspect scheduled worker execution and the board-specific acquisition outcome. For restricted sources, retain the restriction state and retry through the permitted scheduled path; do not bypass access controls. For processing failures, repair and version the parser/extractor, retain original evidence, and create a new valid acquisition/processing revision. An attachment exceeding the declared or streamed eight-MiB acquisition limit reports `processing_failed` with `disclosure_source_too_large`; inspect the official source and supported bounded alternatives rather than treating it as a missing fetch. No empty artifact is fabricated, and previously available retained evidence remains usable for historical inspection. For identity ambiguity, resolve canonical issuer/Listing identity before retrying. For immutable conflicts, investigate ID/provenance reuse rather than overwriting retained history.

Rollback disables research exposure/Skill/acquisition gates and rolls back application code while leaving additive research tables intact. Preserve retained evidence for diagnosis and replay. Re-enable only after source, identity, lineage and affected-purpose checks pass.

## Verification and limits

Deterministic tests cover public MCP schemas and authorization, both board routes, temporal selection, cursor binding, pagination, correction/retraction, restricted/failed artifacts, extraction, report withholding, and no read-side acquisition. Managed Postgres tests cover persistence parity, immutable replay and restart durability.

Focused verification exercises real OAuth consent and public MCP-to-report composition for both boards; negative cases cover scope denial, cross-principal cursor replay, source/artifact restrictions, whole-store/audit outage redaction, schema branch consistency, and dependent-only withholding. These checks establish the tested contract behaviors, not rollout readiness. The delivery PR's Testing/Evidence section is the authoritative record for exact commands, final outcomes and the complete eight-suite gate; a focused pass must not be read as an all-tests-pass claim.

Live verification fetched and retained one TWSE snapshot (five rows) and one TPEx snapshot (four rows). Official MOPS SPA history/detail responses were also captured for TWSE 2072 and TPEx 4530, with an additional 2330 detail capture. Those captures prove endpoint/field behavior at that time; they do not prove historical coverage, complete attachment discovery, sustained provider health, or rollout promotion. Live-source restrictions must remain visible separately from deterministic test results.
